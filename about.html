ABOUT RAILCONNECT

A simpler way to explore rail travel.

RailConnect is an academic front-end project designed to bring useful railway information together in one place. The website demonstrates how passengers can explore train listings, sample routes, station facilities and journey enquiries through a clear interface.

The project focuses on accessible navigation, responsive design and simple interactions using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
